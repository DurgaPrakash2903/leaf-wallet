# Leaf Wallet 🌿

A mobile-first expense tracker that runs in the browser and installs like an app.
Track your bank balance and cash separately, and keep a record of money you've
lent or borrowed so nothing gets forgotten.

## Features
- Sign up and log in (stored on your device)
- Separate bank balance and cash in hand
- Add, edit and delete expenses and income
- Monthly and per-category budgets with red warnings when you go over
- "Safe to spend today" daily limit
- Lent and borrowed money with due dates, part payments and reminders
- WhatsApp reminders and a shareable "who owes me" summary
- Split a bill equally with friends
- Recurring expenses (rent, subscriptions, EMIs)
- Savings goals with progress and confetti
- Custom categories
- Spending trends for 7 days and 6 months
- Light and dark mode, optional 4-digit app lock
- Backup and restore, CSV export
- Works offline, installable on Android and iPhone

## Run it
Open `index.html` in a browser, or host the folder on GitHub Pages.

## Privacy
All data is stored in your browser's local storage. Nothing is sent to a server.
Clearing browser data deletes it, so use Backup and restore regularly.
